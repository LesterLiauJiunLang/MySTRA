Terms and Conditions

Effective Date: June 20, 2026

Platform: Malaysia Short-Term Rental Accommodation System (MySTRA)

1. Purpose of the Platform

MySTRA is a centralized Government-to-Business (G2B) compliance and enforcement gateway operated in alignment with the Visit Malaysia 2026 (VM2026) campaign and UN SDG Goal 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities)[cite: 1]. The platform facilitates legal registration, tax compliance enforcement, and municipal licensing validation for Short-Term Rental Accommodation (STRA) operators across Malaysia[cite: 1].

2. Operator Registration & Account Responsibilities

  • Operators must provide accurate, current, and verifiable business credentials during registration[cite: 1].
  • You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and password.
  • Existing registered businesses migrating from legacy systems must complete the legacy account linkage process prior to verification[cite: 1].

3. Document Submission & Verification Standard

  • To qualify for an active status, operators must upload valid documentation including:
    • Companies Commission of Malaysia (SSM) business registration[cite: 1].
    • Local Municipal Operation License (e.g., DBKL, MBPP, or equivalent local council permit)[cite: 1].
    • Inland Revenue Board (LHDN) tax registration/return confirmation[cite: 1].
  • Submitting fraudulent, altered, or expired documents will result in immediate rejection, permanent system blacklisting, and potential legal prosecution under statutory Malaysian laws[cite: 1].
  • Documents are subject to manual administrative review and verified location proximity enforcement checks[cite: 1].

4. MySTRA Verification Code & Online Travel Agencies (OTAs)

  • Upon official administrative approval, the system issues a unique MySTRA Verification Code[cite: 1].
  • In accordance with government regulatory frameworks, partner Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) operating in Malaysia (including Agoda and Booking.com) validate this code before allowing rental listings to go live[cite: 1].
  • Revocation or suspension of an operator's approval status in MySTRA will automatically signal OTAs to block or deactivate corresponding marketplace listings[cite: 1].

5. Tax Revenue & Statutory Compliance

By using MySTRA, operators acknowledge their legal obligation to adhere to statutory taxation policies, including the 8% Sales and Services Tax (SST) and statutory Tourism Tax requirements where applicable[cite: 1]. MySTRA tracks and shares regulatory status metrics with LHDN and relevant local authorities to eliminate unlicensed operations[cite: 1].

6. Proximity Reporting & Public Auditing

MySTRA provides features allowing live device GPS coordinate tagging and public reporting of suspected illegal or non-compliant properties[cite: 1]. Unverified operations flagged via municipal proximity checks may be subjected to on-the-ground municipal inspections[cite: 1].

7. Modifications & Termination

The administrators of MySTRA reserve the right to modify these terms, update mandatory registration requirements, or terminate non-compliant operator accounts at any time to preserve public safety and urban housing availability[cite: 1].
